## 4. The personal data we collect

### 4.1 Data you give us
- **Account:** email address and password, or your Google account (name, email, profile picture); full name, state, school (optional), phone number (optional); target exams, JAMB subjects and target score; **year of birth**.
- **Parent or guardian:** if you are 13 to 17, the email address of a parent or guardian.
- **Profile photo** (optional), shown on leaderboards and the winners wall only if you allow it.
- **Practice content:** answers, flags, bookmarks; questions you ask the AI tutor, photos of questions you send it, voice questions (turned into text, see 4.3); reactions and reports about AI answers.
- **Challenges:** challenge entries and answers.
- **Payments:** the plan or item you buy, the amount, any discount codes and the payment reference. Card, bank and USSD details are entered with **Paystack** or **Google Play**, not with us.
- **Support:** messages you send us by email or WhatsApp.
- **Invitations:** your referral code, and the fact that a friend joined with it.

### 4.2 Data collected automatically
- **Usage:** tests started and submitted, time used, screens used; videos and notes opened and your progress; ads seen, clicked and rewarded (counts per day); notifications received.
- **Device and app:** app version, Android version, phone model, language and time zone; push notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging).
- **Identifiers for security and fair play:** Android ID and the device's DRM (Widevine) identifier, **stored only as one-way hashes**, to enforce one account per phone and stop cloned apps; whether the phone looks like an emulator, a cloned app or a second user profile. We treat these hashes as personal data.
- **Fair-play signals during challenges and mock exams:** leaving the app, split screen, screenshot or screen-recording detection (Android 14 and later), and time away from the exam.
- **Advertising identifier** (Android advertising ID), read by Google AdMob when ads are shown (see section 8).
- **Diagnostics:** crash and performance data collected by the Google AdMob and Firebase software in the app.
- **Logs:** IP address, request times and error logs kept by our hosting providers for security and troubleshooting.

### 4.3 Microphone and photos
- **Microphone:** used only when you tap the microphone in the AI tutor. Your phone's speech recognition service (on most phones, Google's) turns your speech into text, and may send the audio to that provider to do so. We receive only the text, never the audio.
- **Photos:** only the photos you choose to send the AI tutor or upload as your profile photo. We do not read your gallery.

### 4.4 Data we do not collect
We do not collect precise location, contacts, SMS or call logs, health data or biometric data.

## 6. Children and teenagers

- The Service is for **secondary-school students aged 13 and older** preparing for JAMB, WAEC and NECO.
- **Under 13:** accounts cannot be created. If the year of birth shows a student is under 13, the account is deleted at once and nothing is kept.
- **13 to 17:** the student gives the email address of a parent or guardian. We email the parent or guardian a welcome notice with a plain summary of this policy and the Terms, and a button to **turn the account off**. If they turn it off, the student can no longer log in and the account is deleted after 30 days. We keep a record of each notice (who, when, which policy version).
- **Ads for under-18s:** never personalised; general-audience content only (Google's "teen" setting).
- **Cash prizes** are switched off. If they are switched on in future, they will be for students aged 18 and over only.
- Parents and guardians can contact privacy@solutiontee.com at any time to review, correct or delete their child's data.

## 8. Advertising

### 8.1 Who sees ads
Free users may see Google AdMob ads: banners, native "card" ads, full-screen break ads between screens, "app open" ads when returning to the app, and **rewarded videos** that you choose to watch. **Premium users see no ads.** Ads are never shown during a running exam, CBT test, challenge, mock exam, checkout, or sign-up and account setup.

### 8.2 Rewarded ads
Watching a rewarded video can give you more AI prompts, XP, a streak freeze, or open a Premium note, year or item for a limited time, as shown before you watch. Rewards are confirmed by Google's server-to-server verification. **Rewards have no cash value** and cannot be exchanged for money.

### 8.3 Personalisation
Ads are **personalised only for users aged 18 or over who turn on "Personalised ads"** in Me → Settings. It is off until you turn it on, and you can turn it off at any time. Everyone else gets non-personalised ads. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads. Users in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom also see Google's consent message where required.

### 8.4 Ad frequency and first days
Ad frequency may vary depending on your account, usage, available ads and the content you open. New students may see fewer ads, and may get temporary bonus access to extra practice items, during their first hours in the app before the standard free limits apply.

### 8.5 Our own brand campaigns
We may show sponsored banners, cards, full-screen ads and short video ads for brands we work with, including before, during or after our video lessons. We share **only totals** (views and clicks) with the brand; **no personal data goes to the brand**. Brands must give us their advertising approval (ARCON) before a campaign runs, and only age-appropriate educational or general-audience products are shown.

## 9. International transfers

Our main database is in the **United Kingdom**, and some providers process data in the **United States, the European Union and other countries** (see section 7). When data leaves Nigeria we rely on the transfer grounds in NDPA Part VIII and GAID 2025 Article 18: adequate protection in the destination, contractual safeguards (standard contractual clauses or the provider's data processing terms) and, where needed, your consent or the performance of our contract with you. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. The risk of a transfer is that the destination's laws may give different protection; we reduce this with the safeguards above, and you keep all your rights under this policy.

## 11. Your rights

Under the NDPA you can **access** your data and get a copy, **correct** it (most of it in Me → Edit profile), **delete** your account and data, **object to** or **restrict** some processing, **withdraw consent** at any time (for example notifications, leaderboard photo, personalised ads), receive your data in a common format (**portability**), and **complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission** (ndpc.gov.ng) at any time, without first using our own process.

To use these rights, email **privacy@solutiontee.com**. We reply within **30 days** and may ask you to confirm your identity.

**Delete your account:** in the app (Me → Settings → Delete account), or without the app at **https://cbt.solutiontee.com/delete-account**. We delete your account and personal data within 30 days and keep only what the law requires (payment records) and security records that stop banned users from returning (hashed identifiers only).

## 12. Security

Encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS); row-level security so each student reads only their own data and locked content is never sent to a device that may not open it; private file storage with short-lived links; two-step login for staff; payment and webhook verification; audit logs of staff actions; secrets kept only on our servers. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects you, we will tell you and notify the NDPC within 72 hours where the law requires.

## 13. Notifications and emails

We send service emails (sign-in codes, receipts, security notices, parent notices) as part of the Service. Push notifications (reminders, challenges, news) can be switched off in the app (Me → Settings) or in Android.
