# Refund Policy

- **Effective date:** 8 November 2026
- **Last updated:** 8 November 2026

This policy is part of our [Terms of Service](/terms). It does not take away any right you have under the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Act 2018 or other consumer laws.

## 1. Prices

- The full price is shown in Naira **before you pay**, including any payment processing fee (shown as its own line) and any tax required by law.
- Premium is paid **once** for the period you choose. It **does not renew** and nothing is taken from your account later.
- Buying Premium while you already have it adds the new period after the current one ends.

## 2. When we refund

We refund in full:
- **duplicate charges** reported within 30 days;
- payments where **access was not delivered** and the problem cannot reasonably be fixed, reported within 30 days;
- where a paid item is **materially defective** or does not match its description (or we fix it, if you prefer).

**Our 48-hour guarantee for Premium bought with Paystack:** ask within **48 hours** of paying, and if you have opened **fewer than 3 Premium items** (any mix of locked past-question years, video lessons or solutions, lesson notes or downloadable PDFs), we refund you in full.

Otherwise, Premium and single items are not refundable once used, except for duplicate charges, technical failure, or where the law requires a refund.

## 3. How refunds work

- **Paystack payments** (the app from our website and our payment website): email **support@mail.solutiontee.com** from the email on your account, with the payment reference from your receipt. Refunds go back to the way you paid, usually within **5 to 10 working days** depending on your bank.
- **Google Play payments:** refunds follow **Google Play's refund policy**. Request them in the Google Play Store (Order history) or contact us and we will help.
- When a refund is made, the Premium or item it paid for is removed.

## 4. Discount codes and free days

Discount codes and free-Premium-days codes have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money. If a payment that used a code is refunded, the code use is not given back unless we decide otherwise.
