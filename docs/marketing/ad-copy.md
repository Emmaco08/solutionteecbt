## Posting tips
- TikTok and Reels: post the 9:16 file. Keep the caption's first line as the hook; the video already has the words on
  screen, so it works with the sound off.
- Facebook and Instagram feed ads: the 4:5 file. Run A and B against each other first (same budget, 3–4 days); keep the
  one with the lower cost per install and test C next.
- WhatsApp Status: the `-share` copies are smaller (they send faster and WhatsApp compresses less).
- Every end card carries the disclaimer: SolutionTee CBT is an independent exam-practice platform and is not affiliated
  with or endorsed by JAMB, WAEC or NECO.
