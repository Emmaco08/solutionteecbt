## 4. The personal data we collect

### 4.1 Data you give us
- **Account:**
  - email address and password, or your Google account (Sign in with Google: name, email, profile picture);
  - full name, state, school (optional), phone number (optional);
  - target exams, JAMB subjects, target score;
  - **year of birth** (required at sign-up).
- **Profile photo** (optional). It is shown on leaderboards and the winners wall only if you allow it.
- **Practice content:**
  - answers, flags, bookmarks, notes;
  - questions you ask the AI tutor, photos of questions you send it, and voice questions (turned into text on your phone, see 4.3);
  - reactions and reports about AI answers.
- **Challenges and prizes:** challenge entries and answers. If you win a cash prize: bank name, account number and account name, and any information needed to confirm your identity for the payout **[CONFIRM: any KYC required]**.
- **Payments:** the plan, XP pack or item you buy, the amount and the payment reference. Card, bank and USSD details are entered with **Paystack** or **Google Play**, not with us.
- **Support:** messages you send us by email or WhatsApp.
- **Invitations:** your referral code, and the fact that a friend joined with it.

### 4.2 Data collected automatically
- **Usage:**
  - tests started and submitted, time used, screens used;
  - videos and notes opened and your progress;
  - ads seen, clicked and rewarded (counts per day);
  - notifications received.
- **Device and app:**
  - app version, Android version, phone model, language and time zone;
  - push notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging).
- **Identifiers for security and fair play:**
  - Android ID and the device's DRM (Widevine) identifier, **stored only as one-way hashes**, to enforce one account per phone and stop cloned or duplicate apps;
  - whether the phone looks like an emulator, a cloned app or a second user profile.
- **Fair-play signals during challenges and mock exams:** leaving the app, split-screen, screenshot or screen-recording detection (Android 14 and later), and time away from the exam.
- **Advertising identifier** (Android advertising ID), read by Google AdMob when ads are shown (see section 8).
- **Logs:** IP address, request times and error logs kept by our hosting providers for security and troubleshooting.

### 4.3 Microphone and camera/photos
- **Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO):** used only when you tap the microphone in the AI tutor. Your phone's speech recognition service (on most phones, Google's) turns your speech into text. We receive only the text, never the audio.
- **Photos:** only the photos you choose to send the AI tutor or upload as your profile photo. We do not read your gallery.

### 4.4 Data we do not collect
We do not collect:
- precise location;
- contacts;
- SMS or call logs;
- health or financial account data, beyond prize payout details;
- biometric data.

## 6. Children and teenagers

- The Service is designed for **secondary-school students preparing for JAMB, WAEC and NECO**, most of whom are **13 to 19** years old.
- **Age:** we ask for your year of birth when you sign up. Students who joined before we asked are asked once.
- **Under 18:** we never show personalised (interest-based) ads. Ads are non-personalised and limited to general-audience content (Google's "teen" setting).
- **Under 13:** ads are requested as **child-directed** (Google's "child" setting: no personalised ads, family-safe content only). Brand (sponsored) campaigns are not shown.
- **Parental consent [CONFIRM]:** under NDPA s.31, processing the data of a child (a person under 18) requires the consent of a parent or guardian. Our lawyer will confirm the mechanism, for example:
  - a parent/guardian consent step at sign-up for users under 18; or
  - a minimum age for accounts with a parental consent statement; or
  - verifiable consent for under-13s only.

  Until confirmed, see the open question in `00-README-for-lawyer.md`.
- **Prizes:** cash-prize challenges may be limited by age and by law. See the Contest and Prize Rules.
- Parents or guardians can contact us to review or delete their child's data (section 11).

## 8. Advertising

### 8.1 Who sees ads
Free users may see Google AdMob ads:
- banners;
- native "card" ads;
- full-screen break ads between screens;
- "app open" ads when returning to the app;
- **rewarded videos** that you choose to watch.

**Premium users see no ads.** Ads are never shown during a running exam, CBT test, challenge, mock exam or checkout.

### 8.2 Rewarded ads
- Watching a rewarded video can give you more AI prompts, XP, a streak freeze, or open a Premium note, year or item for a limited time, as shown before you watch.
- Rewards are confirmed by Google's server-to-server verification before they are given.
- **Rewards have no cash value**, cannot be exchanged for money and cannot be used for cash-prize challenge entries.

### 8.3 Personalisation
- Ads are **personalised only for users aged 18 or over**, and only where consent has been given where required.
- Users under 18 get non-personalised ads, and child-directed treatment if under 13.
- Adults can reset or delete their advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in Android Settings → Google → Ads.
- **[CONFIRM: whether to show Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent message to all users, which is required for users in the EEA/UK and recommended under NDPA.]**

### 8.4 Our own brand campaigns
- We may show sponsored banners, cards, full-screen ads and short video ads before, during or after our video lessons for brands we work with.
- We count views and clicks per campaign and share **only totals** with the brand. **No personal data goes to the brand.**
- Brand campaigns are not shown to users under 13.

## 11. Your rights

Under the NDPA you can:
- **access** your data and get a copy;
- **correct** inaccurate data (most of it you can edit in Me → Edit profile);
- **delete** your account and data (Me → Settings → Delete account, or see the account deletion page);
- **object to** or **restrict** some processing, and **withdraw consent** at any time (for example notifications, profile photo on leaderboards, personalised ads);
- **data portability:** receive your data in a common format;
- complain to the **Nigeria Data Protection Commission** (ndpc.gov.ng).

To use these rights, contact **[CONFIRM: privacy email]**. We reply within **30 days**. We may ask you to confirm your identity.

**Account deletion:**
- **In the app:** Me → Settings → Delete account.
- **Without the app:** **[CONFIRM: https://solutiontee.com/delete-account]**, or email us from the address on your account.
- We delete your account and personal data within 30 days. We keep only what the law requires (payment records) and security records that stop banned users from returning (hashed identifiers only).

## 12. Security

- Encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).
- Row-level security in our database: each student can read only their own data, and locked content is never sent to a device that may not open it.
- Private file storage with short-lived signed links.
- Two-step login for staff.
- Payment and webhook verification.
- Audit logs of staff actions.
- Secrets kept only on our servers.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects you, we will tell you and the NDPC as the law requires (within 72 hours to the NDPC where required).
