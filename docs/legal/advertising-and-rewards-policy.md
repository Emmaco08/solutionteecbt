# SolutionTee CBT — Advertising and Rewards Policy

**Draft for legal review. Not yet in force.** Items marked **[CONFIRM]** need a decision by SolutionTee or its lawyer. This Policy forms part of the Terms of Service.

- **Last updated:** 8 November 2026

## 1. Why there are ads

Ads keep a free version of SolutionTee CBT available. **Premium users see no ads.**

## 2. Where ads appear

Free users may see Google AdMob ads:
- **banners**: at the top of some screens, and one "sticky" banner just above the bottom tab bar;
- **native card ads** between items in lists, on Home, in News and inside lesson notes;
- **full-screen break ads**, mostly video, at natural breaks: after a test, before or after a video, after a note, quiz or AI chat, when opening a Study section, and occasionally when moving between screens;
- **app-open ads** when you come back to the app after a while;
- **rewarded videos** that you choose to watch.

The number and frequency depend on the ads level set by SolutionTee, with minimum gaps and daily limits.

**Ads never appear** during a running exam, CBT test, challenge, mock exam or checkout.

## 3. Sponsored (brand) campaigns

- We may show our own sponsored content for brands: banners, cards, full-screen picture or video, and short video ads before, during or after our video lessons. All are clearly labelled **"Ad"**.
- These use the same slots, limits and red lines as AdMob ads.
- They are not shown to users under 13.
- Brands receive only total views and clicks, never personal data.

## 4. Rewarded videos

4.1 Watching is **always your choice**. Before the video we show what you will get:
- more AI prompts;
- XP;
- a streak freeze;
- or opening a Premium note, item or exam year for a limited time.

4.2 Some rewards need several videos (progress is shown).

4.3 There is a daily limit on rewarded videos, and daily limits on how many items or years can be opened by watching.

4.4 The reward is given **only after Google confirms the video was watched** (server-side verification). Closing the video early gives no reward.

4.5 Rewards have **no cash value**, cannot be exchanged for money, and **cannot be used for cash-prize challenge entries**.

4.6 Only years and items that SolutionTee chooses can be opened by watching. Everything else stays Premium.

## 5. Your age and ads

- **18 and over:** ads may be personalised based on your interests, where you have given consent where required.
- **13–17:** non-personalised ads only, with general-audience content limits.
- **Under 13:** child-directed ads only (no personalisation, family-safe content).

We use the year of birth you give us to set this.

## 6. Your choices

- Go Premium for no ads.
- Adults can reset their advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in Android Settings → Google → Ads.

## 7. First-day experience

New students may see fewer ads during their first hours in the app. **[CONFIRM: whether to disclose this here; it is not a hidden charge, and counsel may prefer a general statement that ad frequency can vary.]**

## 8. Honest use

Do not click ads you are not interested in, use tools to fake views, or ask others to click. Accounts that do may be banned. This also protects our ad account.
