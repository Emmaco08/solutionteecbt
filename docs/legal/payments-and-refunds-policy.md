## 1. What you can buy

| Item | What you get | Where you can buy it |
|---|---|---|
| **Premium plan** (for example 1 month, 3 months, or "Exam season" until a set date) | Premium features and no ads until the end date shown | Google Play version: Google Play. Website version: Paystack |
| **XP pack** | XP added to your wallet (spend in the app) | Website version only (Paystack) |

Prices are in Naira and shown before you pay. **[CONFIRM: VAT and whether prices include it.]**

## 2. How payments work

2.1 **Google Play version:**
- payments go through **Google Play Billing**, under Google Play's terms;
- refunds for Play purchases follow **Google Play's refund policy**, and you can request one from Google Play.

2.2 **Website (direct) version:**
- payments go through **Paystack**: card, bank transfer, USSD, or Pay with Bank / apps such as OPay, PalmPay, Kuda;
- we never see or store your full card number, PIN or bank password.

2.3 Access is given **only after the payment is confirmed** by Paystack or Google on our server. If a payment is still pending (for example a bank transfer), access starts when it is confirmed.

2.4 **Premium plans do not renew automatically.** **[CONFIRM: if Play subscriptions are introduced, add renewal and cancellation terms.]** When a plan ends you return to the free version. Your data stays.

2.5 Buying a new plan while Premium is active **extends** your end date. A plan that would not extend your current end date cannot be bought.

## 3. XP

- XP is a **virtual in-app currency with no cash value**. It cannot be refunded for money, transferred, sold or exchanged for cash.
- Bought XP and XP from ads can be spent in the app but **cannot be used for cash-prize challenge entries**.
- If XP is switched off, balances are kept.

## 4. Refunds (website / Paystack payments) **[CONFIRM all]**

4.1 **Duplicate or wrong charges:** if you were charged twice, or charged with no access given, tell us within **30 days**. After checking with Paystack we refund in full or give the access.

4.2 **Premium:** you can ask for a refund within **48 hours** of purchase if you have not used Premium features substantially, for example opened fewer than **[CONFIRM]** Premium items. Otherwise Premium is not refundable, except where the law requires it.

4.3 **Single items and XP packs** are not refundable once opened or spent, except for duplicate charges, technical failure or where the law requires.

4.4 Refunds go back to the original payment method through Paystack, usually within **5–10 working days** depending on your bank. When a refund is processed, the Premium, item or XP it paid for is removed.

4.5 Your rights under the **Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Act 2018** are not affected.

## 5. Failed and cancelled payments

If you leave or cancel a payment before paying, nothing is charged and it is marked "Not finished". If your bank still completes it later, it counts and access is given.

## 6. Promo codes

- Codes have the conditions shown: percentage or Naira off (Paystack only), or free Premium days.
- Each code can be used once per student unless stated.
- Codes have no cash value and may expire.

## 7. Chargebacks and fraud

If a payment is reversed by your bank or found fraudulent, we remove what it paid for and may suspend the account.
