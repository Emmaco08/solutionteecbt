# SolutionTee CBT — Contest, Prize and Referral Rules

**Draft for legal review. Not yet in force.** Items marked **[CONFIRM]** need a decision by SolutionTee or its lawyer. These Rules form part of the Terms of Service.

- **Last updated:** 8 November 2026

## 1. What these Rules cover

- **Challenges & events**: timed, skill-based quiz competitions in the app. Some offer prizes:
  - cash;
  - Premium days;
  - badges or XP.
- **JAMB mock challenges**: full mock exams, including ones run with tutorial centres using a code or invite, with rankings.
- **Leaderboards and the winners wall.**
- **Referral rewards**: rewards for inviting friends.

## 2. Skill, not chance

- Every challenge is a test of knowledge and speed.
- Results depend only on correct answers and time. There is **no random draw**.
- Questions, options and order may be shuffled per student only for fairness.

## 3. Who can take part

3.1 Any student with a valid, unbanned account, unless the challenge page says otherwise.

3.2 **Cash prizes:**
- Winners **aged 18 or over** claim themselves.
- Winners **under 18** need a parent or guardian, who gives their name and phone number and receives or approves the prize. **[CONFIRM: whether under-18s may win cash at all, and the minimum age.]**

3.3 SolutionTee staff, their households and people involved in setting a challenge may not win prizes in that challenge.

3.4 **[CONFIRM: any states or places where cash contests are excluded.]**

## 4. Entry

4.1 Most entries are **free**.

4.2 Some challenges may ask for an **entry fee in XP** (shown before you join, "Join for 100 XP?"):
- **cash-prize challenges accept earned XP only**, never bought XP or XP from ads;
- XP entry fees are not returned after a disqualification.

4.3 **Paid entry in money is not offered.** **[CONFIRM: it stays off unless counsel confirms it is lawful, for example under the National Lottery Act 2005 and state gaming laws, and allowed by Google Play's Real-Money Gambling, Games and Contests policy.]**

4.4 One entry per student per challenge. Joining means you accept the challenge's fair-play rules shown on its page.

## 5. How a challenge works

- Questions come **one at a time** with a time limit per question.
- Each answer is marked and timed **on our server**. An answer arriving later than the time allowed (plus a small grace period) counts as no answer.
- You must stay in the app.

## 6. Fair play

6.1 Not allowed:
- another person answering;
- a second device or account;
- split screen or floating apps;
- screenshots or screen recording;
- emulators, cloned or modified apps;
- automated tools;
- sharing questions during a live challenge.

6.2 We look for signs of cheating: very fast or very even answer times, the same phone as another entry, emulators, split screen, screenshot or recording attempts, leaving the app. A flag leads to a **review by staff**, not an automatic result.

6.3 We may disqualify entries, cancel prizes, take back XP or Premium and ban accounts or phones where we find cheating.

6.4 **Appeals:** a disqualified student may send **one appeal within 24 hours** of the result, with a reason. Our decision after review is final, subject to law.

## 7. Ranking and ties

Ranking is by:
1. the highest score;
2. then the shortest total time;
3. then whoever **started earlier**.

Disqualified and banned entries are left out. Ranks are fixed when the challenge closes. A later disqualification moves the following students up.

## 8. Prizes

8.1 The prizes for 1st, 2nd and 3rd place (and any Premium days for the top N) are shown on the challenge page before it starts.

8.2 **A minimum winning score** may apply (shown on the page). If no one reaches it, that prize is not awarded.

8.3 **Claiming a cash prize:**
- the winner must claim in the app **within 7 days** of the close;
- give their bank, 10-digit account number and account name (in the winner's or, for under-18s, the parent's or guardian's name **[CONFIRM]**);
- we may ask for proof of identity (for example NIN) **[CONFIRM: KYC]**;
- **we never ask for a card PIN, card number or password.**

8.4 Prizes are paid by bank transfer, normally within **[CONFIRM: 14]** days after the fair-play review. Payouts above **₦50,000** need two staff approvals.

8.5 Unclaimed prizes after 7 days, or prizes of disqualified entries, may go to the next eligible student or be withdrawn.

8.6 **Taxes:** winners are responsible for any tax on prizes. **[CONFIRM: withholding obligations.]**

8.7 Prizes cannot be exchanged, except that we may replace a prize with one of equal value if needed.

## 9. Winners wall and publicity

- With your permission, we may show winners' first name and initial (or display name), state or school, photo and amount won on the winners wall and in the app.
- We may show a payment receipt with bank details hidden.
- You can ask us to hide your photo.

## 10. JAMB mock challenges with tutorial centres

- Organisers (for example tutorial centres) may invite students with a code.
- Results and rankings are shared with the organiser only as shown when you join **[CONFIRM: what organisers see, e.g. name and score]**.
- Organisers may not charge students through SolutionTee.

## 11. Referral rewards

- Invite friends with your code.
- A friend counts when they verify their phone and finish one practice test on their own phone (or as the admin rules say).
- Rewards (as shown in the app) may include:
  - a Premium discount per friend;
  - free Premium after a number of friends;
  - a cash reward after a larger number, paid after verification.
- Fake accounts, self-referrals and duplicate phones do not count. They may lead to a ban and cancelled rewards.
- We may cap or change referral rewards. Rewards already earned are honoured.

## 12. Changes and cancellation

We may cancel, pause or change a challenge if technical problems, cheating or legal reasons make it unfair. If a challenge is cancelled before it closes, XP entry fees are returned.

**Google is not a sponsor of, and is not involved in, any contest or prize.** Google Play is not responsible for the contests.
