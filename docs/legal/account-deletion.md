# SolutionTee CBT — Delete your account

**Draft for legal review.** To be published at **[CONFIRM: https://solutiontee.com/delete-account]**. Google Play requires this web link in the Data safety section.

This page explains how to delete your **SolutionTee CBT** account (developer: **SolutionTee**) and what happens to your data.

## Delete in the app (fastest)
1. Open SolutionTee CBT and sign in.
2. Go to **Me → Settings → Delete account**.
3. Read the notice, type to confirm, and tap **Delete my account**.

## Delete without the app
- Email **[CONFIRM: privacy email]** (or support@mail.solutiontee.com) **from the email address on your account**, with the subject "Delete my account". Or use the form on this page **[CONFIRM: form or email only]**.
- We may ask you to confirm by a code sent to that email.
- We delete the account within **30 days** and confirm by email.

## What is deleted
- Your profile: name, email, phone, state, school, birth year, photo.
- Practice history, results, bookmarks, notes and progress.
- AI tutor chats and photos you sent.
- XP, streaks, unused Premium time and items. **These cannot be restored or refunded.**
- Push tokens and notification settings.
- Your entries are removed from leaderboards and challenge lists.

## What we keep, and for how long **[CONFIRM periods]**
- **Payment and payout records** (amount, date, reference) for **6 years**, as tax and accounting law requires. They are no longer linked to your profile.
- **Security records** (one-way hashed device identifiers, ban and fraud flags) for up to **2 years**, so that banned users cannot open new accounts.
- Total, anonymous statistics that do not identify you.

## Before you delete
- Claim any prize you have won first. Unclaimed prizes are lost.
- To delete only some data (for example your AI chats), contact us. You can hide yourself and your photo from leaderboards in Me → Settings without deleting your account.
