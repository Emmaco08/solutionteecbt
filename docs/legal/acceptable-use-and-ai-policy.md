# SolutionTee CBT — Acceptable Use and AI Tutor Policy

**Draft for legal review. Not yet in force.** This Policy forms part of the Terms of Service.

- **Last updated:** 8 November 2026

## 1. Using the AI tutor

1.1 The AI tutor ("SolutionTee AI") helps with school subjects and exam preparation:
- explanations and step-by-step working;
- quizzes and new practice questions;
- study plans and reminders;
- links to our notes and past questions;
- for "latest news" questions about exams, short web search results with their sources.

1.2 **It is an AI.** Its answers can be wrong or incomplete. Check important answers with your teacher, textbook or the official syllabus. It is not a substitute for professional, medical, legal or emotional advice.

1.3 **Education only.** The tutor refuses requests that are off-topic, unsafe or against this Policy. If you need urgent help for your safety, contact a trusted adult or the emergency services.

1.4 **Daily limits:**
- free students have a small number of prompts a day;
- they can get a few more by watching a rewarded video, where offered;
- Premium students have a higher limit.

1.5 **Photos and voice:**
- send only photos of questions or notes, and no faces or personal documents;
- voice questions are turned into text by your phone's speech service, and only the text reaches us.

1.6 **Privacy:** do not share your or anyone else's personal information (address, phone number, ID numbers, passwords) with the tutor.

## 2. Not allowed anywhere in the Service

- Insults, threats, harassment, bullying or hate toward anyone, including the AI tutor or staff.
- Sexual content, or anything involving minors in a sexual way. **Zero tolerance**: we report it to the authorities.
- Violent, self-harm, drug or weapons instructions; illegal activity; scams.
- Spam, advertising, or trying to make the AI produce harmful content ("jailbreaks").
- Cheating in challenges or mocks, using several accounts, cloned apps or emulators, or getting around limits, locks or payments.
- Copying, scraping, recording or reselling our questions, explanations, videos, notes or AI answers in bulk.
- Faking ad views or clicking ads dishonestly.
- Breaking any law or anyone's rights.

## 3. What happens if you break the rules

- **Strikes:** abusive or unsafe messages to the AI tutor get a warning. Repeated strikes may pause your AI access for a period, or permanently.
- We may also remove content, cancel results, prizes or rewards, reduce XP, and suspend or ban accounts and phones.
- **Appeals:** a banned student sees the reason and can send **one appeal within 14 days**. Staff review it and reply in the app.

## 4. Reporting

Use the report buttons on questions and AI answers, or contact support@mail.solutiontee.com. We review reports and act on them.

## 5. Staff review

To keep students safe and improve answers, authorised staff may review flagged AI conversations and reports, under strict access controls and audit logs.
