# Social ads pack: three launch-style ads (owner, 16 Nov 2026, D-182)

Brief: very good adverts with strong hook words for TikTok, Facebook, WhatsApp and Instagram, very animated, in the
style of four "app launch videos" the owner liked on X.

## What the references do (studied frame by frame)
1. Warm glow launch (Fynch): white/cream stage with a soft orange glow, a question as the hook with one *serif italic*
   word, words sliding in out of a blur, the product's real UI floating as cards, a cursor clicking, a prompt box being
   typed into, confetti, a dark end card with the site address.
2. Swiss bold (Online Stage, 15 s, vertical): huge tight sans-serif words on flat colour blocks (blue, red, yellow,
   black), a phone with stacked colourful cards, a hard cut on every beat.
3. Dark gold cinematic (Longshot): near-black stage, gold glow, tilted 3D phone, gold four-point stars, a big word with
   a small line above it ("Dive into company *Financials*"), depth of field, a wall of cards.
4. Coral glow (Loop): typing questions in glass search bars, a 3D phone with a ribbon swoosh, a wall of phone screens,
   motion-blurred words.
Shared: a hook in the first second, a change every 0.5 to 1 s on the music's beat, real app screens, one clean end card.

## The three ads (all 30 fps; 1080 × 1920 for TikTok, Reels, Stories and WhatsApp Status; 1080 × 1350 for Facebook and Instagram feeds)
Music and sound effects only (no voice), like the references: most people watch ads with the sound off, so every word
is on screen. Brand colours: navy, blue, gold (gold never as text on white).

### B. "Stop scrolling" (15 s, Swiss bold colour blocks, 120 BPM)
STOP / SCROLLING. / Start *scoring.* → JAMB · WAEC · NECO (one per beat, real logos) → **One app.** phone with four
coloured feature cards → **Practise** (CBT) → **Understand** (AI tutor) → **Compete** (Game Zone) → **Climb**
(leaderboard) → a wall of app screens: **Your whole exam prep.** → end card.

### C. "Screen time into score time" (30 s, dark gold cinematic, 100 BPM)
**Turn screen time** → *into score time.* (notification bubbles turn into question cards) → logo with gold star glints
→ four chapters, each a huge word behind a tilted 3D phone with a small line above it: *real JAMB-style CBT*
**PRACTISE** · *every answer explained* **UNDERSTAND** · *duels, games, leaderboards* **COMPETE** · *live classes with
teachers* **LEARN LIVE** → a wall of tilted screens → end card.

## Honesty
No promised scores; 287/400 is shown as a student's own result on the app's screen. "Free" means free to download with
free practice questions (Premium unlocks more). No Google Play badge until the Play link exists. The disclaimer is on
every end card next to the exam-body logos.
