The 60 s intro (D-164) is dark and voiced; the 30 s short (D-165) is neo-brutalist; the pack (D-182) is three short
type-led ads. These two are **story-led app ads in the Bolt / Gojek style**: Nigerian students on real-looking
footage, 3D cartoon students, and the app doing the work with a cursor, in our navy, blue and gold.

## Real screens
From the app's own screenshot tests (`apps/mobile/test_shots/`), which draw the real widgets with sample data at
phone size: the existing 18 in `video/public/screens/`, plus new shots where needed (AI tutor answer with steps, topic
search list, Home). No mock-ups drawn by hand.

## Google Flow (Pro plan, 1,050 credits on 17 Nov)
- Free stills first (Nano Banana, 0 credits): the 3D cartoon students and the first frame of every footage clip.
  The owner sees them before any video is made.
- Video: 4 footage clips (lamp, celebration, bus, classroom duel) + 2 short 3D-character moves. Drafts in Omni 1.1
  Flash (12 credits per 8 s, 7 per 4 s) ≈ 62 credits; allow one redo each ≈ 125; the 4 hero clips finished in
  Veo 3.1 Fast (20 each) = 80. **Budget: about 200 credits.**
- People are drawn from a description, never from a photo of a real person. No text, logos or phones-with-UI inside
  generated clips (models break text); the real UI is always our own screen laid on top.

## Rules kept
- Real brand files only (logo Lottie, wordmark). **Exam-body logos are used** (owner, 17 Nov: "my lawyer approved
  it", D-183): the unchanged files from `/brand/exam-bodies/` for every exam that is switched on in the app (JAMB,
  WAEC, NECO today). JUPEB has a logo but is switched off, so it is left out until it is on. The not-affiliated
  disclaimer stays on every end card.
- Honest claims: no promised scores; "free" = free to download with free practice; no cash prizes (off by default,
  D-110).
- Brand colours navy #0B1F44, blue #2563B3, gold #F2B233; gold never as text on white.
- Music and sound effects synthesised (no samples, no licences), mastered to −14 LUFS.

## Formats and files
9:16 (1080 × 1920, TikTok, Reels, Stories, WhatsApp Status) first; then 4:5 (Facebook and Instagram feeds) and 16:9
(YouTube, website). Code: `video/src/major/`; audio: `video/tools/audio_major.py`; Flow clips and stills:
`video/public/major/`; outputs: `video/out/major/` (not in git).

## Laptop limits
Rendering is heavy: one render at a time, nothing else running, Remotion concurrency 1–2.
