# Feature ads: one ad, one major feature (owner, 17 Nov 2026, D-184)

Brief: an ad for each major feature, very hooky, the feature explained well, the best animation. Same production as the
social ads pack (D-182): Remotion, 30 fps, 9:16 (TikTok, Reels, Stories, WhatsApp Status) and 4:5 (Facebook and
Instagram feeds), music and sound effects only, every word on screen, real app screens, real logos, the disclaimer on
the end card, no promised scores.

Each ad (about 19 s) runs: **hook** (0–1.6 s) → **the problem** → **the feature, step by step** (3 beats) → **the payoff**
→ **end card** with a feature-specific call to action.
